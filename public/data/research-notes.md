# Northstowe responsibility map — updated evidence register
Reviewed 12 September 2026. The parish outline is complete. Exact responsibilities and current handovers remain incomplete.

## Road review
Homes England: Phase 2 main roads and the access road from B1050, per the current Northstowe contact page and the Town Council reporting guide. County: 6 adopted road/path records intersect the parish; Eagle Way and Wellington Road are in the prospective Section 38 layer. The repeat county query returned the same records. Road colour represents a contact/scope, not legal width or a blanket claim about adjacent land.

## Public-bin emptying
Anglian Water dog-bin collections are recorded in March 2025 minutes; Greenbelt’s Western Park contractors are recorded in draft March 2022 notes. Both are historical evidence, not verified current schedules. Five OSM bin points in Phase 2 have no recorded operator. Elsewhere only a land/facility enquiry route is shown. Check council service stickers and obtain the actual bin inventory.
