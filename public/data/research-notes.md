# Northstowe responsibility map — research and gap register
Research checked 11–12 September 2026.

The complete ONS parish boundary is displayed. Responsibility evidence remains incomplete. A public source identifying an organisation is distinct from a surveyed maintenance boundary. OSM outlines locate assets; they do not establish ownership. County snapshots are dated downloads, not a live feed.

## Public records added
6 adopted-highway records, 2 prospective-adoption records, 50 explicitly county-owned lights, 176 mapped gullies and 11 rights-of-way records intersect the parish. Complete edge features may extend beyond the parish. The four district property records returned relate to small residential holdings and are not treated as public-realm maintenance areas.

## Remaining information gaps
### Exact maintenance and transfer boundaries
Current GIS polygons and dated handover schedules for Greenbelt, Urban & Civic, Anglian Water and every house parcel. The public plans mix existing and proposed management.
Information holder: Greenbelt, Urban & Civic, Anglian Water and housebuilders

### Current road, footpath and lighting adoption
County centreline, Section 38, lighting and gully snapshots are now included (12 September 2026). Still needed: signed adoption and maintenance-period records, exact highway widths, developer light inventories and later transfers. Absence from a county layer is not proof of private ownership.
Information holder: Cambridgeshire County Council and developers

### H4 trees and parcel-specific exceptions
Greenbelt listed H4 open space in September 2025, but the same forum separately said H4 street trees remain with the developer. Identify the exact trees and scope.
Information holder: Greenbelt and Vistry

### Hatton Road drainage ponds
December 2024 forum notes say Urban & Civic retained maintenance. A later completed-transfer record has not been located. Parts lie outside Northstowe parish.
Information holder: Urban & Civic and Greenbelt

### Phase 3B and remaining development land
An indicative Phase 3B outline is included. Still needed: current ownership and maintenance polygons for each retained, sold or transferred plot, including fields and paths west of Phase 1.
Information holder: Homes England and development partners

### Bins, benches, play equipment and street furniture
Asset identifiers, precise points and the split between emptying, cleansing, repair and replacement contracts. A park-level contact cannot resolve every bin or light.
Information holder: Councils, Greenbelt and land managers

### Inholm and Stirling Fields estate schedules
Current estate-manager appointment, scope plans, retained developer assets and transfers; these may differ from the development footprint.
Information holder: RMG, Homes England, Keepmoat and estate companies

### Named owner of the maintained register
Agree a council contact to approve corrections and record changes when assets transfer. No requests have been sent as part of this research.
Information holder: Town Council and partner organisations
